2. 导入 UsagiPass 配置
    - ![](/image/mihomo-qrcode.svg)
    [一键导入 <i-ion-md-rocket /> 配置](shadowrocket://add/sub://aHR0cHM6Ly9keHBhc3MudHVyb3UuZnVuL1VzYWdpUGFzcy55YW1s?remark=UsagiPass)
